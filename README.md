🚗 Electric Vehicle Dashboard
📊 Overview

The Electric Vehicle Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project created using Tableau.
It provides insights into the adoption, growth trends, and performance metrics of electric vehicles (EVs) across various regions, manufacturers, and vehicle types.

This dashboard is designed to help users understand key trends driving the electric vehicle industry, including sales, market share, charging infrastructure, and emission reductions.

🎯 Objectives

To analyze electric vehicle adoption trends over time.

To visualize sales growth and market share by manufacturer and region.

To compare EV types (Battery EVs, Plug-in Hybrid EVs, etc.) in terms of performance and popularity.

To explore charging station availability and environmental benefits.

⚙️ Tools & Technologies

Tableau Public – For creating and publishing the interactive dashboard.

Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset – For storing and preprocessing raw EV data.

Data Sources – EV registration datasets, government reports, or open datasets (like Kaggle or data.gov).

📈 Key Insights

Year-over-year growth in EV sales and registrations.

Market distribution by manufacturer, region, and vehicle type.

Correlation between charging infrastructure and EV adoption rates.

Comparison between EVs and traditional vehicles in terms of CO₂ emissions reduction.

💡 Features

Interactive filters for year, region, and manufacturer.

Dynamic KPIs and trend lines for easy data interpretation.

Geographical map showing EV distribution across regions.

Visual comparison between EV categories (BEV, PHEV, etc.).
