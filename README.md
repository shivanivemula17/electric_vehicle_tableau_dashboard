<h1 align="center">🚗 Electric Vehicle Dashboard</h1>

<h2>📊 Overview</h2>
<p>
The <b>Electric Vehicle Dashboard</b> is an interactive data visualization project created using <b>Tableau</b>. 
It provides insights into the adoption, growth trends, and performance metrics of electric vehicles (EVs) across various regions, manufacturers, and vehicle types.
</p>
<p>
This dashboard helps users understand the key trends driving the EV industry, including sales growth, market share, charging infrastructure, and environmental impact.
</p>


<h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Analyze <b>electric vehicle adoption trends</b> over time.</li>
  <li>Visualize <b>sales growth and market share</b> by manufacturer and region.</li>
  <li>Compare <b>EV types</b> (Battery EVs, Plug-in Hybrid EVs, etc.) in terms of performance and popularity.</li>
  <li>Explore <b>charging infrastructure availability</b> and environmental benefits.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>⚙️ Tools & Technologies</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Tableau Public</b> – For creating and publishing the interactive dashboard.</li>
  <li><b>Microsoft Excel / CSV</b> – For storing and preprocessing raw EV data.</li>
  <li><b>Data Sources</b> – Open datasets from Kaggle, data.gov, or government EV statistics.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>📈 Key Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Year-over-year growth in EV sales and registrations.</li>
  <li>Market distribution by <b>manufacturer</b>, <b>region</b>, and <b>vehicle type</b>.</li>
  <li>Correlation between <b>charging infrastructure</b> and <b>EV adoption rates</b>.</li>
  <li>Comparison between <b>EVs and traditional vehicles</b> in terms of CO₂ emissions reduction.</li>
</ul>


<hr>

<h2>💡 Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Interactive filters for <b>year</b>, <b>region</b>, and <b>manufacturer</b>.</li>
  <li>Dynamic KPIs and trend lines for easy data interpretation.</li>
  <li>Geographical map showing <b>EV distribution</b> across regions.</li>
  <li>Category-wise comparison between <b>BEVs</b> and <b>PHEVs</b>.</li>
</ul>
